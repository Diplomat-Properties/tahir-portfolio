MUHAMMAD TAHIR — GITHUB PORTFOLIO

1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open the tahir-leadership-portfolio folder.
3. Double-click index.html to preview your website.
4. Open your GitHub repository.
5. Upload everything INSIDE this folder. Do not upload the ZIP itself.
6. Confirm index.html is at the top level of your repository.
7. Go to Settings > Pages.
8. Select Deploy from a branch > main > /(root) > Save.
9. Wait for deployment, then use the Visit site button.
